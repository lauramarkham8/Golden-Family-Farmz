## Brand & Style

The design system embodies "Natural Farm Luxury"—a synthesis of grounded Hawaiian stewardship (mālama ʻāina), athletic discipline, and warm culinary elegance. Rooted in Kahaluʻu on the windward coast of Oʻahu, it avoids kitschy tropical tropes (hibiscus prints, tiki motifs, hyper-saturated neon sunsets) in favor of deep Koʻolau foliage, rich volcanic soil, and sun-drenched golden harvest tones.

### Target Audience
Conscious epicures, local Oʻahu community members, farm-to-table culinary partners, and discerning travelers seeking genuine agricultural provenance and high-quality seasonal produce.

### Design Movement
**Organic Editorial Modernism:** A harmonious pairing of high-craft literary typography with generous negative space, warm tactile surfaces, and subtle, structured borders. The interface conveys quiet confidence, legacy, and organic abundance without visual noise.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the windward environment of Kahaluʻu—deep canopy greens, sunlit raw honey, damp rich loam, and crushed natural parchment.

### Application Guidelines
- **Primary (`#294735` - Deep Forest Green):** The cornerstone tone for high-authority typography (h1–h3), global navigation, active interactive states, and solid grounding containers.
- **Secondary (`#D5A43B` - Golden Honey):** Reserved for intentional points of conversion: primary action buttons, delicate decorative rules, active indicator dots, and harvest highlight accents. Avoid using as large surface fills.
- **Tertiary (`#806044` - Earth Brown):** Serves metadata, subheadings, supporting iconography, secondary borders, and agricultural lineage accents.
- **Neutral Base (`#F8F5EC` - Warm Cream):** Canvas base tone that softens the visual field compared to stark digital white.
- **Accent Soft Sage (`#B8C6A6`):** Deployed for organic badge backings, muted section dividers, seasonal tag pills, and input hover washes.
- **Surface Elevation Contrast:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8F5EC`
  - Elevated card surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle inset surface: `#F0EDE2`

## Typography

The type system balances the literary heritage of Windward Oʻahu storytelling with the clarity required for direct farm orders, CSA subscriptions, and event schedules.

- **Newsreader** delivers an authentic, unhurried presence across large displays and narrative section breaks. Italics are reserved for narrative Hawaiian terminology (e.g., *ʻāina*, *mālama*, *kanaka maoli*) and provenance dates.
- **Manrope** provides a geometric yet human body texture with wide open counters, ensuring maximum legibility across harvest lists, order flows, and logistical details.
- **Micro-copy & Badges:** `label-sm` utilizes subtle tracking (`0.06em`) combined with uppercase formatting strictly for agricultural metadata, crop availability statuses, and coordinates.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around expansive breathing room that reflects the open fields of Kahaluʻu.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout; canvas margin `1.25rem`; column gutter `1rem`. Section vertical padding clamped to `space-xl`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout; canvas margin `2.5rem`; column gutter `1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout; maximum structural container width of `1280px`; exterior margin auto-centered with baseline `5rem`; column gutter `2.5rem`.

### Rhythms & Gaps
- Group related items (labels, inputs, sub-items) with `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Card internal padding uses `space-md` on mobile and `space-lg` on desktop.
- Vertical editorial spacing between narrative blocks uses `space-2xl` to maintain a calm, premium browsing cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

To protect the organic, grounded ethos of the farm, synthetic high-cast drop shadows are completely omitted. Depth is generated through layered natural surfaces and warm ambient undertones.

### Techniques
1. **Tonal Planar Surfaces:**
   - Level 0 (Canvas): `#F8F5EC` (Cream)
   - Level 1 (Cards, Product Tiles): `#FFFFFF` (Pure linen white)
   - Level 2 (Flyout Menus, Harvest Cart): `#FFFFFF` with a natural ambient rim
2. **Warm Ambient Shadows:** When floating elements require optical separation from cream backgrounds (e.g., sticky cart bar, quick-view modals), apply soft, loam-tinted ambient diffusion:
   - `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(41, 71, 53, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(128, 96, 68, 0.05)`
3. **Soft Boundary Rules:** Rather than harsh grey dividers, structure content using delicate low-opacity borders:
   - Default border: `1px solid rgba(41, 71, 53, 0.12)`
   - Subtle separator: `1px solid rgba(184, 198, 166, 0.35)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tailored organic architectural feel using subtle, refined radius values. Completely circular pill buttons or razor-sharp brutalist corners are avoided; the goal is the gentle weathering of milled natural timber and river stones.

- **Base Radius (`rounded`, 0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to form fields, tags, and small utility triggers.
- **Medium Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to product cards, testimonial panels, and image frames.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Applied to primary featured hero containers and dialog sheets.

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:**
  - Background: `#D5A43B` (Golden Honey)
  - Text: `#294735` (Deep Forest Green), `label-lg`, medium weight
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#C39228` with subtle transform lift (`translateY(-1px)`)
  - Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`, radius `0.25rem`
- **Secondary (Editorial Ghost):**
  - Background: Transparent
  - Border: `1.5px solid #294735`
  - Text: `#294735`, `label-lg`
  - Hover: Background fills with `rgba(41, 71, 53, 0.06)`

### Chips & Badges
- **Harvest Status Chips (e.g., "Harvested This Week", "Kahaluʻu Grown"):**
  - Background: Soft Sage (`rgba(184, 198, 166, 0.3)`)
  - Text: Deep Forest Green (`#294735`), `label-sm`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(41, 71, 53, 0.1)`
  - Padding: `0.25rem 0.625rem`, radius `0.25rem`

### Cards
- **Product & Story Cards:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(41, 71, 53, 0.08)`
  - Padding: `1.5rem`
  - Radius: `0.5rem`
  - Visuals: Imagery retains a natural warm grade with 3:4 or 4:5 vertical editorial crops. Subtle zoom transition on container hover (`scale(1.02)` within overflow-hidden bounds).

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:**
  - Canvas: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(128, 96, 68, 0.3)`
  - Focus state: Border transitions to `#294735` with a `2px` focus halo of `rgba(213, 164, 59, 0.3)`
  - Typography: `body-md` in `#294735`, placeholder in `rgba(41, 71, 53, 0.4)`

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Indicators:**
  - Base: `1px solid #806044`, radius `2px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio)
  - Selected state: Solid `#294735` fill with honey checkmark `#D5A43B`

### Lists & Produce Rosters
- **Produce Seasonal Schedule:**
  - Horizontal tabular rows divided by `1px solid rgba(184, 198, 166, 0.35)`
  - Hover state creates a gentle `#F0EDE2` wash across the active item.
  - Left-aligned crop name in `Newsreader` (`headline-sm`); right-aligned seasonal harvest window in `Manrope` (`label-sm`).
